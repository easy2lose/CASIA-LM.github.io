DialectS2S

End-to-End Speech Dialogue Modeling for Low-Resource Chinese Dialects

DialectS2S extends OpenS2S to multilingual and low-resource dialect interaction across Mandarin, English, Sichuanese, Cantonese, and the Tianjin dialect. It combines scalable dialect dialogue synthesis with self-aligned speech supervision for more consistent, intelligible, and natural spoken responses.

Self-Aligned Speech Supervision

DialectS2S synthesizes speech targets from the fine-tuned Thinker's text predictions, aligning the Talker's speech supervision with the model's evolved semantic representations during dialect adaptation.
